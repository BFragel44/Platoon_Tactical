# NCM calculations rules audit

Date: 2026-10-18. Scope: active company combat calculations, including Company Assault and Normandy. This is a focused audit, not certification of every combat rule. No gameplay calculations changed in this audit.

## Sources and method

Local `reference/Fields_of_Fire_Rules_3rd_Edition.pdf`: terrain effects (5.2.3, p.36), cover/overcrowding (5.3, p.37), crossfire and misses (6.2.4, p.43), NCM selection and printed examples (6.4, pp.45–46), mortar direct/indirect fire (7.3.2, p.53), grenades (7.10, pp.55–56), concentrated fire (7.11, p.56), and visibility (9.0, p.69).

`scripts/auditNcmCalculations.js` constructs isolated fixtures and calls the production `combatExposure` function. Expected values are authored from the rules, not generated by a second copy of the implementation. These fixtures do not demonstrate an entire legal sequence of orders. They do not load or modify player saves.

Run `node scripts/auditNcmCalculations.js` for JSON; it deliberately exits nonzero while any observation fails. Recorded output: `output/ncm-calculations-audit.json`.

## Findings

### CR-03 — cumulative grenades depend on a scenario setting

**Confirmed calculation mismatch.** Section 7.10.2 says successful grenade VOFs accumulate. Company Assault has no `mission_rules.grenade` setting, but `combatExposure` only combines grenade markers when that setting exists.

Reproduction: two successful −4 grenade markers on one recipient, terrain +3 and cover +1. Expected fire −8, total/NCM −4. Company Assault produces fire −4 and total/NCM 0. The same fixture in Cerisy produces the expected −4. Neutral terrain can hide this defect because both outcomes hit the −4 lower limit.

Location: `src/sim/company/battlefield.js`, `combatExposure`, the `combined` grenade condition. This is a scenario-dependent gap; do not describe all Normandy grenade accumulation as broken.

### CR-04 — temporary direct-lay mortar PDFs omitted from terrain direction

**Confirmed calculation mismatch with synthetic retained-PDF fixtures.** Sections 5.2.3 and 7.3.2 require direct-lay fire to use crossed terrain borders; the mortar PDF remains relevant even when its grenade attempt fails. The production grenade action retains `temporary_pdf` on a missed direct-lay attempt.

Reproduction: high/low terrain protection +2/+0, white north border and dark east border; a temporary mortar PDF enters from the east and a grenade miss remains on the card. Expected terrain +2 and raw NCM +1; actual terrain 0 and raw NCM −1. Adding ordinary S fire from the north gives crossfire −1: expected raw NCM 0, actual −2.

Location: `src/sim/company/battlefield.js`, `combatExposure`. `temporaryMortarFire` contributes to crossfire directions but not `borderFire` used for terrain protection. This audit isolates the resulting calculation; it does not claim an end-to-end authored-order reproduction.

## Passing coverage

47 of 50 observations pass; three failed observations represent the two defects above.

- Printed worked examples on pp.46, 55 and 56, including mixed factions, point-blank fire, pinned fire, grenade misses, targeted grenades and concentrated fire.
- Lowest applicable VOF after smoke, light, weather and burst modifiers; successful grenade and off-map support exemptions.
- Directional terrain: any dark-border fire requires high protection, including weaker basic fire alongside stronger Incoming. Indirect-only and point-blank cases use low protection.
- Critical cover loss even when another VOF is selected; unrelated cover remains protected.
- Overcrowding at three, four and six steps; grenade/Incoming penalties and ordinary on-map indirect treatment.
- Different-bearing crossfire, same-bearing exclusion, indirect exclusion, cumulative concentration, pinned plus exposed, and NCM bounds.

`test/ncmWorkedExamples.test.js` protects the passing printed examples. The audit retains explicit failing expectations for CR-03 and CR-04 rather than asserting the defective outcomes as correct.

## Corrections implemented — 2026-10-19

CR-03 and CR-04 are fixed for newly created missions. Grenade accumulation no longer depends on a scenario-specific grenade strength setting, and temporary direct-lay mortar PDFs participate in the terrain-border calculation as well as crossfire.

New executable replays record `ncm_corrections: "v1"`; mission state retains `ncmCorrections: "v1"`. Missing discriminators retain the historical calculations, including during attempt-start snapshot creation. Unknown correction variants are rejected. Existing frozen exposures and historical replay prefixes are not rewritten. Previously started missions retain their original NCM rules; new missions use the corrected calculations.

All 50 audit observations now pass. `test/ncmCorrections.test.js` covers the full audit, corrected versus historical calculations, exact replay round trips for Company Assault and Cerisy, historical re-export and unsupported versions. Thirteen focused suites pass (147 tests), including historical Normandy compatibility, specialist replay, reattempt cover and reverse-side replay coverage. Production build passes with the existing bundle-size advisory.
