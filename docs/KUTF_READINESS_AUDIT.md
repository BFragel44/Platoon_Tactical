# KUTF readiness audit — transition to campaign work

The subsequent [terrain/cover audit](TERRAIN_COVER_AUDIT.md) corrects the identified border, building-weapon and printed-terrain discrepancies in rules 14 / KUTF content 12.

## Assessment

**No confirmed standalone blocker remains in the audited scope.** Repeated user playthroughs, culminating in “The playthrough was fun,” satisfy the general human-playability gate. Notes 4 exposed display defects rather than incorrect mine persistence or fire-mission duration; those are fixed. Acceptance applies to the documented adaptation, not complete third-edition action-menu coverage.

This pass compared published setup/exceptions (KUTF pp.4–7), placement/expansion/scoring/support tests, mission availability and accumulated checklists. Many remaining-looking entries were obsolete development notes, now separated from current status.

## Remaining work is not a general KUTF gate

No arbitrary test-count target or complete automated-browser playthrough is required. Keep critical regressions and investigate concrete defects. Optional action-menu expansion, final art and exhaustive rare-case certification remain follow-ups. See [current status](KEEP_UP_THE_FIRE_STATUS.md) for adaptation boundaries.

KUTF explicitly omits tracked ammunition/fire-mission counts, vehicles and signals and simplifies communications (p.4). These published exceptions must not be changed to campaign rules.

## Actual next blockers

Normandy cannot be ungated by renaming KUTF or carrying its autosave forward. It needs mission contracts, stable roster identities, deterministic deployment, an idempotent debrief transaction, and Trévières ammunition/support, communications, enemy capabilities, counterattacks and reattempts. See [Normandy readiness](NORMANDY_READINESS.md).
